01 / No previous knowledge needed

So… what is a Space Marine?

A human transformed into a superhuman soldier through implanted organs, intense training, and conditioning. Add a powered suit of armour and extremely destructive weapons, and you have one of humanity's elite warriors.

The world they live in

Warhammer 40,000 is a dark science-fiction setting in the far future. Humanity's vast empire, the Imperium, is threatened by aliens, rebellion, and supernatural powers called Chaos.

The group they belong to

A Chapter is an independent Space Marine brotherhood with its own leaders, traditions, colours, and equipment. Many follow a model of roughly 1,000 fighting Marines, although there are exceptions and supporting personnel.

What makes them different

Chapters share much of the same technology, but have different personalities. One prizes careful planning; another prefers lightning attacks. Think military orders with distinct cultures, rather than different species.

Are they the good guys? They are often the heroes of their own stories, but they serve a cruel, authoritarian empire. “Loyalist” means loyal to the Emperor and Imperium; it does not automatically mean morally good.

02 / The family tree

First. Second. What does “Founding” mean?

A Founding is an occasion when new Space Marine forces are created. It tells you about their origins, not their strength or rank. The first two explain most of the family tree you need to start with.

01 · The origins

First Founding

The Emperor creates the original Legions: enormous Space Marine armies. Each has a genetic connection to a superhuman leader called a Primarch.

02 · The great split

The Horus Heresy

Around 10,000 years before the main 40K setting, Horus leads a devastating civil war. Of the 18 Legions active in this era, nine side with him and nine remain loyal.

03 · The reorganisation

Second Founding

After the war, most loyal Legions divide into smaller, independent Chapters. A Chapter keeps the old Legion's name; other branches take new names as successors.

04 · The setting today

Warhammer 40,000

Many more Foundings follow over the millennia. This guide stays with the nine original loyalist Chapter identities and a small selection of Second Founding successors.

The important distinction: the original forces were Legions. “First Founding Chapters” is the usual name for the Chapters that carry on those original loyalist names and traditions. A successor is its own organisation, not simply a squad in its parent Chapter.

04 / Translate the jargon

You don't need to speak High Gothic.

Open a term for the plain-English version.

Adeptus Astartes

The formal name for Space Marines. In most conversations, the two terms mean the same thing.

Emperor of Mankind

The immensely powerful founder of the Imperium. In the main 40K setting, he is sustained on the Golden Throne and worshipped by much of humanity.

Primarch

One of the Emperor's superhuman creations, each associated with an original Legion. A Primarch is a Chapter's genetic ancestor, not its ordinary commanding officer.

Gene-seed

The biological material used to grow the special organs that help make a Space Marine. It connects a Chapter to its genetic lineage and can pass on unusual traits.

Legion / Chapter / Company

A Legion was a vast army from the earlier era. A Chapter is a smaller, independent brotherhood. A Company is a unit inside a Chapter, often around 100 Marines in the standard model.

Codex Astartes

Roboute Guilliman's guide to military organisation and warfare. Some Chapters follow it closely; others adapt it or depart from it substantially.

Successor Chapter

A Chapter descended from an older Space Marine lineage. It inherits genetic ancestry, but develops its own leadership, traditions, and history.

Chaos / the Warp

The Warp is a supernatural dimension connected to thoughts and emotions. Chaos refers to its destructive gods, daemons, and corrupting powers. The two words are related, but are not exact synonyms.

Firstborn / Primaris

Firstborn are Marines made using the older enhancement process. Primaris are a later development with additional enhancements. These terms describe types of Marine, not which Founding their Chapter belongs to.

Bolter / power armour

A bolter fires powerful explosive projectiles. Power armour is a powered protective suit that supports and enhances its wearer's movements.

Chapter Master

The leader of a Chapter. Some Chapters use different titles or leadership arrangements. A Chapter Master is still a Space Marine, rather than a Primarch.

Librarian / Chaplain / Apothecary

A Librarian is a psychic specialist. A Chaplain guides the Chapter's beliefs and morale. An Apothecary is a battlefield medic who also safeguards gene-seed.

05 / Things everyone wonders at first

A few useful answers.

Were there only nine original Legions?

No. Twenty were originally created. Two were erased from Imperial records, leaving 18 active at the time of the Heresy. Nine became traitors and nine remained loyal. This guide focuses on the nine loyalist lineages.

Is a Second Founding Chapter weaker or less important?

No. “Second” describes its origin, not a lower rank. The Black Templars, for example, are a major force with a very distinct identity.

Are the Grey Knights and Deathwatch missing?

They sit outside this guide's simple family-tree approach. The Grey Knights are a secretive force specialising in fighting daemons. The Deathwatch brings Marines from many Chapters together to fight alien threats. Neither is a straightforward example of the successor relationships introduced here.

Do I need to read the Horus Heresy first?

No. Knowing that a huge civil war split the Space Marines is enough to begin. Learn one Chapter that interests you, then explore its stories. You can return to the Heresy when you want the deeper background.

Does a Chapter's favourite tactic define everything it can do?

No. These are memorable tendencies, not absolute restrictions. Raven Guard can fight open battles, White Scars use more than bikes, and Ultramarines can improvise. Individual characters also vary.
